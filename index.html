ABOUT WHO DIS

WHO DIS is a modern-day take on the classic Guess Who game. Mark has picked a person and so have you. You ask about faces. Mark asks about something else.

Victor Barreto, a developer and retro gaming enthusiast, recreated and developed this game. Click here to learn more about him and explore his other projects.

HOW TO PLAY

Pick your person from the board. Mark secretly picks his.

Each round you ask Mark a question about his person, then he asks you one about yours. Click faces to flip down the ones it can't be.

Ready? Press GUESS and click a face, any time. Get it right and you win. Get it wrong, or run out of rounds, and Mark wins.
